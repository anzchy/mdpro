Motion

#11

Hover, press and switch transitions use --motion-duration-fast (150ms) with --motion-ease-out. Under @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) the duration token is 0ms, so every transition collapses at once.

Not copied from Codex (review §3)

  • No OpenAI Sans and no Codex gray ramp — system sans and the existing mdpro neutrals (D4 decided: keep current neutrals).
  • No black or foreground-coloured primary button — primary is --color-accent.
  • No separate fixed control blue — controls, focus and selection all derive from the one theme accent.
  • Menus keep the solid-accent highlight, not a neutral-step hover.
  • No 46pt in-web title bar or 24px chrome — the 38px drag strip and 36px tab bar stay.
  • No translucent sidebar, superellipse corners, light-dark(), [data-theme] or Tailwind spacing; no 12px body text.
