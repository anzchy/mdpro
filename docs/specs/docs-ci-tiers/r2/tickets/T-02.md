## Parent

#594 (spec r2). Ticket **T-02** (issue #596, revised). High tier, gate-sensitive.

## What to build

**`ci-gate` becomes a tested script with one rule for every job that runs only sometimes (DCT-r2-D9).** Each such job is always in `needs`. It must succeed when the scope outputs say it is required, and it may be `skipped` only when `scope` succeeded and its outputs say it was not needed. A missing or unknown output fails the gate.

**The policy lane.** A `policy` PR, or a `prose` PR touching a Gate input, runs `prose-checks` and `doc-policy`. `doc-policy` runs exactly the readers under its own vitest config, without coverage, with a collected-count check (D10). The PR skips `frontend` and `rust-test`.

**The runtime trace (D6)** in the `frontend` run fails on a read of a prose path that is not a known Gate input.

**Timeouts.** `frontend` and `rust-test` go to 45 minutes (D16).

**Records.** The "policy lane and fail-closed gate" entry lands with a new `#pull-request-ci` subsection of rule 70, and ADR-0095 decision 3 gains its "folded and changed" line.

Spec: DCT-r2-D6, D9, D10, D16, D20; stories 10–12, 15, 23, 25.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC-1 (unit, seam 3, behavioural): for each of `frontend`, `rust-test` and `doc-policy`, the gate script:
  - passes required-and-`success`;
  - fails required-and-`skipped`;
  - passes not-needed-and-`skipped`;
  - fails `skipped` when `scope` failed or its output is missing or unknown;
  - fails any `failure` or `cancelled`.

  It also fails unless `scope` and `prose-checks` are `success`.
- [ ] AC-2 (unit, seam 3): `ci.yml` lists `scope`, `prose-checks`, `frontend`, `rust-test` and `doc-policy` in `ci-gate`'s `needs` and calls the gate script. `rust-audit` is not in `needs`.
- [ ] AC-3 (unit, seam 2): `doc-policy`'s config includes exactly the scan's reader list, `*.slow.test.ts` readers included. The job fails when the collected count differs from the list.
- [ ] AC-4 (unit, seam 2): the trace check, on fixture traces, fails on a traced read of a prose non-Gate-input path, naming the path and the reader. It passes on Gate inputs, policy and code paths.
- [ ] AC-5 (CI): on the PR's own run, the trace step runs inside `frontend` and is green on the committed tree.
- [ ] AC-6 (unit, seam 3): `frontend` and `rust-test` have `timeout-minutes: 45`.
- [ ] AC-7: the entry, the rule 70 `#pull-request-ci` subsection and ADR-0095's dated line for decision 3 land in one commit. The entry is indexed and was plan-reviewed by both auditors.
