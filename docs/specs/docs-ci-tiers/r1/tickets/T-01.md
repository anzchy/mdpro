## Parent

#594 (spec, snapshot `docs/specs/docs-ci-tiers/r1/` once committed). Ticket **T-01**. High tier, gate-sensitive.

## What to build

A single classifier answers `prose`, `policy` or `code` for any repo-relative path, and both the CI scope step and the landing command's docs-only mode use it. A PR's change scope is the most demanding answer across its paths; an empty or unreadable range is `code`.

In this ticket `ci-gate` still treats `policy` exactly like `code` (full CI). The CI skips arrive in T-02, so this ticket alone can never weaken CI. The direct docs-only commit accepts only `prose` paths. Rule 70 § Commits item 2 is rewritten to the change-scope route, in the same commit as the "change scopes" decision-log entry, and ADR-0095 decision 2 gains its "folded and changed by" forward link.

Spec: DCT-r1-D1, D2, D3 (prefix part; the scan-derived exact paths come in T-02), D5 (landing mode), D15 (change-scopes entry); stories 1–3, 13, 14, 35–38.
