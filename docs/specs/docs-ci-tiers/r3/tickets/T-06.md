## Parent

#594 (spec r3). Ticket **T-06** (issue #600, revised). High tier, gate-sensitive.

## What to build

Every test file that spawns `pwsh`, directly or through a `tests/` helper it imports, moves to a `*.slow.test.ts` sibling (DCT-r2-D17).

- **Harnesses.** Helpers that spawn it are split, so that non-slow files keep importing only the non-spawning parts.
- **The lint** refuses a direct or transitive spawn in a non-slow file.
- **The slow-trigger set** is derived from what the moved `pwsh` files import or spawn; other slow files do not set `slow`.
- **The `slow` job.** The scope step sets `slow=true` on a PR touching that set, and a `slow` job in `ci.yml` is then required through T-02's gate rule.
- **Nightly.** `slow-nightly.yml` runs the whole slow lane nightly and on dispatch. `ci.yml` keeps no `schedule` trigger.
- **The unit inventory.** `tests/ci-inventory.json` is updated for the moved files.

**Records.** The "slow lane in CI" entry lands with rule 10 § Environment and the slow tier's new membership criterion. ADR-0050 gains its dated line ("Current text: `10-tdd.md#environment-and-the-slow-tier`; folded and changed by …"), and ADR-0095 decision 7 gains its line for "the slow lane runs locally on push" and "no check moves into the PR run".

Spec: DCT-r2-D17, D20; stories 26–29.
