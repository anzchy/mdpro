## What to build

Proof on real work, with no code. The delegator confirms that two items each ran `jack-tdd-workflow` end to end:
- the first gated Claude item **granted after both** 0.3.6 shipped and T-02 landed. The delegator holds that grant until both are true;
- the first Codex item granted after T-02 landed that runs a full test matrix. A one-cycle fix round does not count.

This is a process check, not a `unit` or `device` AC under ADR-0144. It has no brief and no PR. The delegator records each confirmation as a row in `docs/audit/audit-log.md` in a docs-only commit, then closes this issue after the second row. Any gap it finds becomes its own fix item, not an edit here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For each of the two items, the audit-log row names:
  - the item id and its grant time, and T-02's landing SHA;
  - the attacker, with evidence that it was a fresh dispatch (not a fork, not resumed), and the attack file's path;
  - the first cycle's receipt id and its decisive assertion-failure line;
  - the `verify` HELD output.
- [ ] Each row shows that the item ran in cycles. From `receipts.json` and the evidence sidecar, each `<ID>-S<n+1>` was recorded after `<ID>-S<n>`'s `verify` settled. An `<ID>-ACC` receipt, if any, comes before `S1`.
- [ ] Every gap found is registered as its own item, linked from this ticket.
