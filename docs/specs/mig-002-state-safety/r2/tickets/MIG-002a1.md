## What to build

**MIG-002a1: the App journey harness, proved by two save clauses.** This is the tracer bullet: the first App journey that goes from a keystroke in the real `<App />` to bytes in the write log, plus the refusal path that proves a failed flush writes nothing.

- **The journey harness** renders the real `<App />` with real Milkdown and real stores over MIG-001a's fake Tauri boundary. It adds to that boundary:
  - the webview quit event bus, with `quit_guard_reply` capture;
  - window close requests;
  - `menu:action` delivery;
  - dialog fakes (ask, message, save, open) answered by script;
  - the IPC commands the App calls at startup and in journeys;
  - a `file:changed` emitter with the origin window label;
  - an MCP event emitter;
  - updater and process stubs.
- **Fail-closed is a harness layer.** Its dispatcher throws on any unmodelled command. MIG-001's shared boundary is not changed and not relied on.
- **Harness helpers:**
  - typing and IME composition;
  - fake time with `advance(ms)`;
  - StrictMode as an option;
  - `readTokens(id)`;
  - the **IPC call log**;
  - the empty **fake-behaviour table**;
  - the **transition recorder**;
  - the real-serializer break and heal (D92's shape, promoted);
  - teardown checks: the Milkdown ctx timeouts are drained by the existing timer drain, then no other pending timer, live listener or registered flusher may remain.
- **The known-limitation registry** (one file per row) and its check test, which rejects `ref: pending` and malformed records.
- **Clauses** (spec #733):
  - a-C2 (path 1): Cmd+S before the edit-sync delay elapses writes the latest typed text.
  - a-C4 (path 1): a failed flush writes nothing, does not mark the document clean, and is never reported as success.
  - a-CS (pin): the serializer throws during a save.

State owners: row a's (spec § Contract clauses). StrictMode clauses: a-C2. ## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Harness self-tests pass:
  - a save records exact bytes;
  - an unedited open records no write;
  - the IPC call log records a non-write command with its arguments;
  - a scripted dialog answer reaches the app;
  - a quit reply is captured;
  - an unmodelled IPC call throws;
  - a journey that leaves a non-Milkdown timer, a listener or a flusher behind fails, while the Milkdown ctx timeouts are drained;
  - the real serializer break yields `failed`, and the heal restores it;
  - `readTokens` reports a known delta;
  - the transition recorder records each notified state;
  - Milkdown mounts under fake timers.

  If Milkdown can't mount under fake timers, the fallback goes to the user through the delegator before any further journey is written.
- [ ] The corpus suite (MIG-001) stays green, with its boundary behaviour unchanged.
- [ ] The handoff reports the harness's mount cost per journey.
- [ ] No card: row a's card is a2's.
