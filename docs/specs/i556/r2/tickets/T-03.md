## What to build

The landing command also requires device evidence bound to what lands. It reads the verifier record's DEVICE line:
`none`, or each device AC with its cited report and that report's releaseId SHA. Each cited report must exist under the
device folder and carry the line `IDENTITY releaseId <version>-dev@<sha40>`, with the same SHA. That SHA must have
ADR-0144 decision 5's identity with the landing SHA:
- no change between them inside the identity tree, which is the whole tree minus `docs/` other than `docs/test/cards/`,
  minus `tests/` other than `tests/manual-fixtures/`, and minus `.claude/`, `.agents/`, `.codex/` and `CHANGELOG.md`;
- each cited card's blob unchanged.

The device-brief template is amended so that every report carries the IDENTITY line. The AC-text hash, and whether the
report says PASS, stay with the verifier. Both are recorded as limits.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A DEVICE line of `none` adds no requirement.
- [ ] Cited reports that exist, whose IDENTITY SHA is identical in identity to the landing SHA, let the landing proceed.
      A docs-only change between the two counts as identical.
- [ ] A cited report that is missing, has no IDENTITY line, or whose IDENTITY SHA differs from the DEVICE line is
      refused, naming the device check.
- [ ] A change inside the identity tree, or to a cited card, between the release SHA and the landing SHA is refused.
- [ ] An unparseable DEVICE line is refused.
- [ ] The verification-record template's DEVICE line, and the device-brief template's IDENTITY line, match the pinned
      grammar (fixture test).
- [ ] Proved at the landing-command process seam.
