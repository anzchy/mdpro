## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC-1 (device): with mdpro closed, `RegisterHotKey` for Ctrl+Shift+F returns true, and `UnregisterHotKey` follows at
  once. Both results are recorded.
- [ ] AC-2 (device): in the installed build, a real Ctrl+Shift+F press toggles the sidebar, and `mdpro.log` shows
  `ui.shortcut.trigger` with `view.toggle_sidebar`. The View menu shows Ctrl+Shift+F.
- [ ] AC-3 (device): Ctrl+Shift+L is recorded as owned elsewhere on this PC: `RegisterHotKey` returns lastError 1409,
  and no trigger is logged for a real press.
- [ ] AC-4: the build identity (SHA-256 and the `app.ready` `releaseId`) matches the locked rerun build. Config
  isolation is restored, and its hash is recorded before and after.
